## Grounding decisions

- All prose is a bilingual teaching adaptation; no passage is presented as the complete original text. Explicitly new contexts use kind `依据单元目标新编` (starter notebook, unit 4 town guide, unit 6 torch shopping, unit 7 health vocabulary). Other passages use `依据课文的教学改写`.
- Ben/Lucy/Buster are the Book 3 protagonists. The clue sequence is I, F, R, S, E, then N and D. Seven letters rearrange to FRIENDS, while Horax tries FINDERS. The two words use the same letters. The conclusion sends the treasure to a museum.
- Unit 8 is a model park, not an actual same-day world tour. The INDIA label loses N and D; I, I, A remain. The story explicitly identifies Mr Williams as Horax in disguise at the finale.
- Unit 2 golden apple healing is a fictional plot device, labelled as such. No animal first-aid instruction is provided.
- Unit 5 final puffin sighting is Christine’s belief; identity is not objectively established. The course preserves this uncertainty. The children's care actions are narrated as events, not generalized wildlife advice.
- Do not repeat the book’s old claims about “world’s fastest car”, “biggest plane”, etc. The grammar uses stable invented comparisons with an explicit scope instead.
- Unit 6 historical summary is not repeated as a precise history of who invented numbers/Fibonacci sequences. The mathematics activity is independently checkable: IV = 4 and 1,1,2,3,5,8,13.
- Unit 7 avoids the old source’s overly narrow fever explanation, threshold-based treatment and alcohol thermometer project. The explicit new vocabulary scaffold teaches temperature terms and communicating symptoms, without diagnosis or medication decisions.
- Unit 8 ancient Babylon height/dates and the pyramids’ dating are not used as current facts. Origami activity is based on the source folding directions.
- Unit 9 says “many places”, not all countries have four seasons; no universal wet-season month claim is copied. North-up maps are specified as the map in the lesson, not a rule for all maps.
- No original CD listening answers or performances have been invented. Song tasks direct learners to the source song page and separate computer speech from original music. Pricing in the new shopping passage is explicitly made up for the task.
- Grammar exactly follows the contents/appendix: good at + -ing; possessive ’s; likes; have to; some/any; suggestions; clock time; adverbs; position; purpose infinitives; was/were; comparisons; past forms; did/didn’t; going to. Two points and four questions per unit.
- Phonics follows the contents targets. Word pairs and MCQs are new practice; accents are noted for /r/ and /ɜː/. The exercise tests phoneme patterns rather than claiming an automatic pronunciation score.
