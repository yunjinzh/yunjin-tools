## Source handling and deliberate teaching changes

1. Every story and reading is labelled `依据课文的教学改写`. The starter world passage is explicitly `依据单元目标新编`. No claim of full verbatim reproduction.
2. Even-numbered units (2,4,6,8) contain functional language dialogues in the original book, not songs/phonics tracks. Their `song.title` explicitly starts 功能对话, and `phonics.note` says the spoken-reading activities are newly written. Odd units and starter keep the source song titles and phonics goals, using original new practice examples; no original recordings implied.
3. The children’s encounters with Elvis/Shakespeare and the time-gate explanation for the Mary Celeste are explicitly fictional. The final school-time discrepancy remains an open question; no made-up “true answer” is assigned.
4. Story danger is summarized rather than turned into a child activity: no chemical recipe, no climbing activity, no weapon/confrontation task. Projects use paper games, drawings, safe ordinary objects and explanation. The lesson still preserves cause-and-effect and actual plot.
5. p16 dated disaster cost/death rankings are omitted. p43 the broad “Mozart’s music has 60 bpm / improves memory” claim is not reproduced. p66–67 mining-production rank, investment-like claims and outdated proportion statistics are omitted. p114–115 the book’s incorrect Sea of Azov separation and old maximum depth/mountain height values are not propagated.
6. p64 culturally broad statements are made specific: different Indigenous communities have different languages/homes, teepees and bison are not assigned to everyone. The source title’s older term is explained. The inaccurate assertion that 1492 was the first European arrival is omitted.
7. p29 remains explicitly a 2008 article, without claims about current numbers of isolated groups. The task emphasizes land, dignity and respecting choices.
8. p76 origin countries are attributed to the example room in the text, not asserted as present-day country-of-origin facts for every product.
9. p54–55 keeps food-category language without personal diet amounts or body-size judgement. No nutritional/medical advice added.
10. p112 sea mystery keeps reports distinct from guesses; aliens are not asserted as an established cause. No sensational missing-person statistics needed.
