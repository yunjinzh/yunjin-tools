# Title webfonts

The webfonts are subsets of Adobe Source Han Serif SC, version 2.003.
Official source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif/release/Variable/OTF/SourceHanSerifSC-VF.otf
Original SHA-256: 24980e3fdbdf7cbef800133c9bc8937cb65533ca50f0bd0565115db496f57220

Copyright 2017–2022 Adobe. Licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; the complete copyright notice and license are in OFL.txt.

The original glyph outlines are retained. The font was subset to this site's characters and instantiated at weights 400, 500 and 600. The modified primary family is named Stratagem Serif SC to respect the reserved font name. No external font service is required. Future added text should trigger regeneration of the font subsets using scripts/build-title-fonts.py.
